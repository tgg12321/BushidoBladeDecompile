Mid-body region splice, not a whole-body replace: the prologue and the tail call survive; the body

body instruction or the tail-call spelling silently mis-fires the delete.** The 393C8 failure mode
(+452 bytes, body duplicated, caught only by the oracle rebuild) applies verbatim here. Plan
"re-anchor or retire in the same change" from the first edit.
