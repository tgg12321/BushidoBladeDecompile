## 6. Judge-risk flags

1. **The park reason is factually wrong and the Judge will see it.** "NO cheat on main,
   clean floor-4 candidate retained" is contradicted by `regfix.txt:2495-2525` (9 rules) and
   by two un-annotated `do { } while (0)` constructs at `src/text1b.c:6461-6464` and
   `:6477-6481`. The grind session must lead with this correction; a Judge that discovers it
   independently will treat the whole submission as unreliable.
2. **Unrelated secondary cheats the park reason never mentions.** (a) The empty
   `do { } while (0)` barrier at `:6461-6464` — sanctioned family, but un-annotated and
   here used as a bare scheduler barrier, and the 2026-06-14 reviewer already FAILed this
   function's do-while forms (`memory/wip/func_80072CD4/meta.json` reviewer.evidence).
   (b) `s32 new_var;` (`:6450`) — a multi-write carrier (`0xFC` constant holder at `:6473`,
   then an `(s32)arg1` address holder at `:6490`) with a coercion-announcing name; multi-write carriers are explicitly
   NOT covered by the named-intermediate entry (the `y1` FAIL,
   `.claude/rules/no-new-park-categories.md:205-207`). (c) `regfix.txt:2517`
   `insert_after "addiu\t$2,$zero,70"` is a **lost-codegen insert**, its own named cheat
   family. All three disappear with the dup4 transplant — but only if the transplant is
   complete; a partial edit that keeps `new_var` or a barrier will FAIL.
3. **Species check.** The ruling sanctions duplicating common-tail stores into both arms.
   The dup4 form does exactly that (stores present in both arms, absent from the merge). It
   does **not** additionally rely on any store-order regfix `reorder` — that rule is being
   retired, not preserved — so the sanctioned construct is genuinely load-bearing rather than
   decorative. Verify this holds after the transplant: if the build still needs the
   `reorder`, the F7 form is not actually closing and the submission is a different animal.
4. **Directionality caveat, quoted from the survey** (`tmp/sotn_family_surveys.md:509-511`):
   "SOTN's instances read as *preserving* a redundancy the authors believe the original code
   had ('Bug? Code is the same in both paths'), whereas BB2's F7 *hoists* stores that are
   genuinely common. Both produce a C form whose only justification is the bytes, but the
   framing differs and the owner may weigh it." The owner did weigh it and sanctioned the
   construct anyway; cite the ruling text, not the survey, as authority.
5. **The mechanism-scope objection survives in the survey but not in the ruling.** The survey
   verdict is literally "ESTABLISHED (construct); the mechanism-scope objection survives". A
   default-FAIL Judge re-reading the survey could stop at that headline. The ruling closes it
   explicitly ("sanctioned at the CONSTRUCT level regardless of which GCC pass the
   duplication feeds") — quote that sentence in the commit message.
6. **Residual the family cannot close:** none expected — dup4 is a measured 0 at 79/79. If
   the rename wave or neighbouring edits in `src/text1b.c` have moved the baseline, the
   fallback is `candidate.c` at floor 4, which is INCOMPLETE (and would still be a strict
   improvement over main: it removes 9 rules' worth of debt but breaks the oracle, so it is

7. **All-or-nothing commit.** Because the 9 rules currently hold the byte match, the body
