## SECOND LEVER

If the transplant leaves a small residual, the two degrees of freedom are (a) **the `"r"` operand
expression** and (b) **which GPR preamble instructions live inside vs. outside the block**. Target
wants `addiu $v0,$sp,0x10` + `addu $t4,$v0,$zero` (island 1), `addiu $v0,$sp,0x30` + `addu $t4,$v0,$zero`
(island 2), `addiu $s0,$s0,0x354` + `addu $t4,$s0,$zero` (island 3) — i.e. GCC materialises the address
into a scratch register and the macro copies it to `$12`. So pass the plain array/pointer (`mat`,
`vec`, `arg0`) as the `"r"` operand, never a pre-computed cast held in a named local, and keep
`arg0 += 0x354;` as a real C statement so `addiu $s0,$s0,0x354` is compiler-emitted.
Do **not** hoist the `lhu`/`sll`/`or` pack arithmetic out into C on the theory that it is "more pure":
target keeps it *between* `addu $t4` and `mtc2` inside the macro body, and the FDB0 authorization line
explicitly covers "the move/lw/nop scaffolding those macro bodies carry" — while equally explicitly
bounding it: "in-island GPR instructions authorized ONLY as the addressing preamble of a cop2 transfer
with no C form", and "an island grant never licenses per-instruction GPR asm outside a granted island".
