## 1. What the rules do — REGION blob on SLOT-ORDINAL anchors

```
func_80070C70: rename "{lbl#1}" ".L80070D7C"
func_80070C70: rename "{lbl#7}" ".L80070EEC"
func_80070C70: delete_between "^\.L80070D7C:$" "^\.L80070EEC:$"
func_80070C70: insert_before  "^\.L80070EEC:$" "<~110 instructions>"
```

The two `rename` rules pin cc1's 1st and 7th function-local label slots to stable names; the
`delete_between` then removes everything between them and the `insert_before` supplies target's
text. So the blob covers the **two draw loops in the middle of the function** — the prologue, the
first `SetDrawMode`/`AddPrim` block and the tail calls are genuinely compiled.

Anchor fragility: **FRAGILE (slot-ordinal).** `{lbl#N}` survives cc1's *file-global* counter moving
(that is what the rename mechanism is for, and it has already survived several such shifts), but it
does NOT survive a change in how many labels this function emits before slots 1 and 7. **Any loop
restructure, added `if`, or changed break/continue shape renumbers the slots** and the blob lands
somewhere else. Failure is silent-ish (the rename resolves to the wrong label, then delete_between
removes the wrong span), so **verify with a full clean build + SHA1, not just the sandbox.**

**True starting distance: the 118 is real** in the sense that the C body is complete and covers the
blobbed region (both draw loops are written). It is inflated by cheat-asm the sandbox strips
(below), so 118 is the honest number for the C as written.

## 5. Levers

**First lever — delete the cheat-asm and re-measure the honest floor before any codegen work.**
Replace the `la` with `&D_800A3590`, the seven `addiu %0,$0,N` constants with plain literals, and
the `asm("$20")` pin with an ordinary local. This is required for completion anyway, it is
score-inert-to-better under the honest sandbox, and it tells you what the real starting number is.
Expect the floor to move; 118 is the score of a body written to feed a blob, not a body written to
match.

**Second lever — [[phantom-slot-frame-lever]] on the `frame-stack-offset` ×14 cluster.** With 34
spills and a large local `PrimC70`/`IconC70` pair, the residual is a frame-layout question: does
target's frame reserve bytes ours does not, or is it a slot-assignment rotation? The
`.frame vars=` gradient procedure ([[phantom-frame-slots-gcc272]]) is the diagnosis, and the frame
census locator exists. Note the campaign map's caution: **contiguous runs of rules are usually ONE
finding counted N times**, so do not read 14 rules as 14 problems.

**Third — the struct declarations.** `PrimC70` and `IconC70` are decomp-authored types; if their
field offsets or sizes are wrong the whole frame layout is wrong downstream.
[[header-type-correction-from-use-sites]] is the four-prong gate for correcting them from use sites.

**Before touching the loops**, remember the slot-ordinal anchors: any change to loop shape
renumbers `{lbl#1}` / `{lbl#7}`. If the loops need restructuring, plan to convert the blob's anchors
to the robust `.frame` / `.end` directive form (as used by E6B0/EB84/CA8C) in the same change — see
