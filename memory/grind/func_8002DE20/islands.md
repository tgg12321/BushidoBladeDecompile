  tools/maspsx_empty_offset_prototype.diff: `[^(]+` -> `[^(]*` in the base+offset regex and an
  empty offset read as "0") passes `($12)` through unchanged to GNU as. With it, the fully
  verbatim-text body compiles to a code6cac_b.o `.text` byte-identical to the `0($12)` body:
