codegen divergence. Same for the two `lw` islands at `8002FD74`/`8002FD8C`.

**The comment's premise is false, and there is an in-repo counter-exhibit.** `func_80023D28`

and its target emits precisely `lui $at, (0x1F8001E0 >> 16)` + `sw …` (`asm/funcs/func_80023D28.s`,
